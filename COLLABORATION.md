# Collaboration statement

I worked individually on this project; I did not have teammates. The definitions, theorem
statements, overall organization, and video are my own work. I used AI tools for assistance
with parts of the Lean proofs, with build fixes, and with editing the report and README text;
the tools used, the files and theorems involved, and the kind of help are described in [`AI.md`](AI.md).


Outside human help:
- Sidharth Hariharan (21-321 TA) and the course staff gave feedback on the project, including the feedback on API design (preferring Mathlib's `IsChain` and separate hypotheses over wrapper predicates) discussed in the report.
- Prof. Sumun Iyer (instructor of 21-329 Set Theory, Spring 2026) taught the course that gave me
  the background on trees, ordinals, and omega_1 that this project builds on. I did not collaborate with her on the formalization itself, but I met with her to discuss the original
  scope of the project, possible approaches, and possible extensions toward Suslin trees.
